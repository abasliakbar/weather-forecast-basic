# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyone who wants to check weather for a city or their current location.

## Product Purpose

WeatherNow provides current weather conditions and a near-term forecast so people can quickly understand the weather where they are or where they plan to go.

## Positioning

No meaningfully distinct product mechanism has been confirmed.

## Operating Context

People search for a city or use their device location, then scan current conditions, atmospheric details, and the next 24 hours of forecast data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Search weather by city and retrieve current conditions and forecast data from OpenWeatherMap.
- Use browser geolocation to retrieve weather for the user's current location.
- Show temperature, condition, humidity, wind, feels-like temperature, visibility, and an hourly forecast.
- Support dark and light themes.
- Preserve the existing search, weather-data, geolocation, forecast, and theme behaviors during visual redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

- The existing application is implemented in `index.html`, `style.css`, and `app.js`.
- Live weather data is sourced from OpenWeatherMap.
- No customer testimonials, benchmarks, or other product claims are established.

## Product Principles

- Make current conditions and near-term changes quick to understand.
- Keep city search and location-based weather straightforward.
- Present weather measurements clearly and accurately.
- Preserve existing capabilities while improving the experience.
